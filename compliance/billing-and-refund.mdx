## 5. Subscription Cancellation & 7-Day Refund Policy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Frictionless Self-Service Cancellation">
    Users can cancel recurring renewals anytime from their Account Settings with zero penalties. Access remains fully active through the end of the paid billing cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Statutory 7-Day 100% Refund (Zero Usage)">
    Under applicable e-commerce consumer protection regulations (such as Article 17 of the Republic of Korea E-Commerce Act), if zero AI pulses have been consumed within 7 days of purchase, users are entitled to a **100% full refund** by emailing `billing@connexbrothers.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Non-Refundable Scope After Resource Allocation">
    Once AI pulse consumption initiates or high-performance GPU/TPU computing resources have been allocated, partial or prorated refunds for the remaining cycle duration are not provided due to real-time cloud resource consumption.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Abuse Mitigation">
    Refund privileges are forfeited if accounts are terminated for automated abuse, scraping, or breach of platform security terms.
  </Step>
</Steps>
