## 4. Subscription Renewal, Transitions & Annual Discount

1. **Advance Billing Cycles**: Subscriptions renew automatically on the same calendar day each month or year unless cancelled at least 24 hours prior to renewal.
2. **20% Annual Commitment Discount**: Annual commitments grant an upfront 20% discount ($1,920.00/yr for CONNEX X, $768.00/yr for CONNEX B).
3. **Instant Tier Upgrades & Proration Credit**: Upgrading between plans takes effect immediately. Unused pulse balances from a previous CONNEX P Credit Pass ($20.00) or an active B plan are mathematically settled and credited against the new upgrade invoice.
4. **Scheduled Downgrades at Period End**: Downgrading to a lower plan incurs no immediate fee ($0) and maintains 100% of your current plan benefits and pulse quotas until the end of your active billing period. At cycle end, unused pulses expire as fully delivered for that period without mid-cycle cash friction, and the lower plan rate automatically initiates for the subsequent billing cycle.
5. **Annual Commitment Mid-Cycle Cancellation, Discount Clawback & Cap Guard**: For annual subscriptions cancelled prior to completing 12 months:
   - **Zero Penalty on Unserved Months**: No cancellation fee is charged for unserved future months.
   - **Discount Clawback**: Used months ($M$) are reconciled at the standard Regular Monthly Rate ($80.00/mo for B / $200.00/mo for X).
   - **Formula**: `Refund = Annual Payment - (Months Used M × Regular Monthly Price)`.
   - **Two Protective Caps**: Refund amount is always $\ge \$0.00$ (no reverse-billing), and total discount clawback cannot exceed one month of the regular monthly fee ($80.00 or $200.00), protecting users who utilized 10+ months from forfeiture.

Plan benefits, features, and remaining pulse quotas remain 100% active until the end of the paid billing cycle, after which pulses expire as the monthly contract is fulfilled in accordance with consumer regulations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Statutory 14-Day 100% Refund (Zero Usage)">
    Under applicable e-commerce consumer protection regulations (such as Article 17 of the Republic of Korea E-Commerce Act and FTC Cooling-Off rules), if zero AI pulses have been consumed within 14 days of purchase, users are entitled to a **100% full refund** by emailing `billing@connexbrothers.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fair Usage-Deducted Prorated Settlement (Partial Usage Within 14 Days)">
    If pulses were consumed within the 14-day window, users may still request a statutory refund. Only the fair cost of actually consumed pulses (pulses consumed × pulse unit rate) is deducted, and the remaining balance is refunded directly to your original payment card via Stripe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-14-Day Terms">
    After 14 calendar days, monthly subscription fees are non-refundable due to dedicated cloud resource allocations and run until period end. Annual plans cancelled mid-term qualify for prorated clawback settlement under Section 4.5.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Abuse Mitigation">
    Refund privileges are forfeited if accounts are terminated for automated abuse, scraping, or breach of platform security terms.
  </Step>
