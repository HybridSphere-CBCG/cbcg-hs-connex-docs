## Fair Use Policy (FUP) & Cognitive Capacity

For the **CONNEX X** flagship plan, the allocation of **1,000,000 Pulses per month** delivers practically unlimited cognitive freedom. 

Based on deep workflow analytics:
- An intensive enterprise user averages 5 minutes per turn (prompting, deep model reasoning, and brief validation).
- Even under maximum physiological commitment (15 hours/day awake, dedicated deep work), human capacity peaks at approximately **360 turns per month (~476,000 to 720,000 pulses)**.
- **1,000,000 Pulses provides ~63 hours (757 turns) of continuous dialogue**, ensuring authentic human users never experience throttling or quota anxiety.
